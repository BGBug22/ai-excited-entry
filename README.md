## The image

A giant, brightly coloured wooden cuckoo bird smashes its way out of a cuckoo clock in a clockmaker's workshop, wings spread, beak open, bursting straight toward the viewer. Gears, springs and clock hands fly everywhere. Below, the tiny clockmaker grandmother screams and ducks for cover, her cap flying off.

Taped to the workshop bench is a handwritten note for the judge:

> dear judge, i only wound it once. sorry about your screen! love, nia (7)

One hero, one gesture, one absurd moment — a small craftsman, an impossible job, and an explosion aimed right at whoever is looking.
